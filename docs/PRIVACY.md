Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Standwise app ("Standwise", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Standwise is a work and standby (on-call) time tracker for teams.

## What we collect

- **Account data:** when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password.
- **Profile:** the first and last name you enter in the app.
- **Organizations:** the organizations you create or join, your role (owner, admin or employee) and your membership status.
- **Time data:** your time entries and running timer: start and end times, duration, type (work or standby), project, description, and the hourly rates you enter, as well as monthly totals calculated from them.
- **Projects and settings** created in an organization (names, clients, colours, currency, CSV format).
- **Invitations:** the email address and role of people an admin invites.
- **Activity log:** a record of administrative actions in an organization (for example who changed a role, and when).
- **Technical data:** our hosting provider processes technical data such as IP addresses and device information to run and secure the service.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or health data. We show no ads and do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

## Why we use it

- To provide the app: sign-in, time tracking, reports, CSV export and team features (performance of our contract with you).
- To let an organization's owners and admins see the hours and amounts of their members, as the organization requires (performance of a contract, or the organization's legitimate interest).
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interest).
- To send invitation emails that an admin requests.

## Who can see your data

- **You** see your own entries, rates and reports.
- **Owners and admins of your organization** see your name, email, role, time entries, rates and amounts in that organization. They cannot edit your entries.
- **Other members** of your organization see your name in member lists. They do not see your entries.
- **Service providers:** we use Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) for sign-in, database and server functions. Data is stored in the European Union (Firestore multi-region eur3, Cloud Functions in europe-west1). Google may process some data outside the EEA under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep it

- Your data is kept while your account exists.
- Deleted time entries can be restored for 30 days; after that they are permanently erased.
- When you **delete your account** (Settings → Profile → Delete account), we delete your profile and memberships and sign you out. Time entries you recorded in an organization stay there without your name and email, so the organization keeps its records of paid work; the organization owner can choose to have them deleted instead.
- When an owner deletes an organization, all of its data is deleted.
- Backups and logs at our hosting provider are deleted on their regular schedule.
